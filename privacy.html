Color Vision Plates — Privacy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

The screening runs on your device. It does not submit your answers to the server. The website uses pageview analytics and server request logs, as described below.

The iOS app

The bundled screening runs offline and does not upload answers. Screening history is stored on your device. You can erase it with Settings → Clear Screening History. The bundled page disables web analytics. Opening an external link or choosing to share a link uses the destination app or website and its own privacy policy.

The web version

Answers are scored in your browser. After a completed run, the page saves a result category, label, and timestamp in local storage under whatcolor_test_result. The companion What Color app on the same website origin can read that summary. The Android availability banner also remembers dismissal locally. You can remove this storage by clearing the website's data in your browser.

The screening page loads GoatCounter pageview analytics from stats.dr.eamer.dev. The screening code does not send answers or result summaries to that service. Web-server access logs can contain request information such as IP addresses and user agents.

External links and sharing

Sharing is initiated by you and shares a link to the screening. External sites, including the companion app and references, have their own privacy policies. The screening does not require an account or email signup.
