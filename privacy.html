Color Vision Test — Privacy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Short version: your screening results stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking, on any platform.

The iOS app

The Color Vision Test iOS app collects no data at all. The entire test is bundled with the app and runs offline — the app makes no network requests. Your answers and screening history are stored only on your device, and you can erase them anytime with Settings → Clear Screening History. There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs.

The web version

The web test at whatcoloristhis.one/test/ runs entirely in your browser: answers are scored on your device and are not uploaded. The website uses self-hosted GoatCounter for privacy-respecting, cookie-free page-view counts (no personal data, no cross-site tracking). Standard web-server logs (IP address, user agent) are kept briefly for operational purposes, as with nearly every website.

What this means in practice

  • No personal information is collected, sold, or shared.
  • Screening results never leave your device on any platform.
  • Links to external sites (the companion app, methodology sources) open in your browser; those sites have their own policies.
